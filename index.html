⚠ The honest headline

The two strategies rest on three years of backtest, which is about one and a half market cycles. The standard error of a three-year Sharpe ratio is roughly ±0.7, so the breakout rule's measured 1.10 carries a t-statistic of about 1.5 — below the bar this project set for itself.

That is a hint, not a proof. No amount of re-reading the same three years changes it. Only elapsed time with the decision already committed does, which is what the forward record is for and why it started on 10 August 2026.

Portfolio engine live

Holds positions over time rather than measuring trades in isolation. Point-in-time universe selection, correct one-day offset, fees and funding charged on every position.

Ships with a strategy that cheats on purpose — if it stops reporting an absurd number, the look-ahead guard has broken.

Risk engine live

Single-market, correlated-group, gross-notional, daily-loss and drawdown limits — checked in code before every order, never remembered by a human.

The group limit is the important one: twenty alt-coins in a selloff is one bet in twenty wrappers, and a per-position limit cannot see it.

LONA-RISK.bat

Execution pipeline dry run

Agent wallet, kill switch that latches, arming that always expires, and an append-only audit log that records the refusals as well as the orders.

Dry run runs the identical code path with the last step off, so a week of its log is a real prediction rather than a story about one.

008 · Breakout momentum

Rank the perpetuals by trailing 30-day dollar volume, re-rank weekly, take the top 20. When one closes at a new 50-day high, hold it five days at 1/20 of capital. Otherwise cash.

Dividing by the size of the universe rather than by the number of signals is deliberate: a quiet market leaves you in cash instead of putting the whole account into one coin.

Character: a bull-market engine with a cash brake. Positive in 4 of 7 years. In the last bear it returned −3% at a −8% drawdown while Bitcoin fell 48%. It protects capital; it does not print money on demand.

009 · Hedged funding carry

Rank the top 40 by trailing 7-day funding, re-rank monthly, short the ten richest payers on the perpetual and hold an equal size on spot, so the price cancels and only the funding is kept.

Unhedged this loses 62% a year — the coins paying the most funding are the ones going up hardest, so shorting them without the spot leg is shorting momentum.

The Sharpe of 11 is not a real Sharpe. Carry income arrives in a trickle and the loss arrives all at once, from something not in the sample: venue failure, a delisting while short, a liquidation on a wick, a collateral depeg.

⚠ What these numbers do not contain

Three years is one and a half cycles. Both t-statistics are borderline and the breakout's is below the bar.

The carry needs a second venue. Hyperliquid's own spot book does $61M a day against $1.14B on its BTC perp alone, so the hedge cannot live there at size — it means two sets of fees, two collateral pools and transfer risk between them.

This is research, not advice. Every number above is a backtest. Losses are possible and leverage makes them faster.

Ten results, seven of them dead

The dead ones are kept on purpose. The record of being wrong is what makes the record of being right worth anything.

✓ The method, where every rule was earned by a death

1. Rank on volatility-adjusted lift, so the do-nothing baseline is exactly zero.  2. Count only independent observations.  3. Require stability across periods, not one lucky split.  4. Record both directions, or the baseline is the market.  5. Check how the sample was chosen before believing anything measured on it.

6. Re-cost at every fee tier.  7. Model the carry, not just the price.  8. Include what died.  9. Run a broken-shift control — deliberately let the strategy see the return it is about to earn, and if that does not produce an absurd number, nothing else in the run means anything.  10. No order placement until the statistics justify it.

Spot venue account next

Order-book research waiting

Nothing to analyse until there is a month of tape. First questions: does touch imbalance predict the next second net of the spread, and do the same counterparty addresses appear before moves?

Cross-venue funding basket waiting

Hyperliquid pays 4–7% a year more than Gate, on the same side, 77–95% of days. Too thin alone; a basket of ten might not be. Needs months, not the 22 days the venues will serve.

A third edge waiting

Two strategies is a thin book. Cross-sectional momentum, tail mean reversion, seasonality, and the Asian-range sweep from the LCE script are all untested on the survivorship-free archive.

Plumbing proven

Create an agent wallet, place one minimum-size order by hand, then let LONA place one behind a command that refuses to exceed a few dollars. Proves the payload, the fills, the fee accounting and the funding maths. Cost of being wrong: a few dollars.

about two weeks

Live at 1–2% of intended capital

Big enough that the fills, slippage and funding are real. Small enough that six months of being wrong costs less than the information is worth. This is the forward test, with skin in it.

three to six months

Size up, or do not

Three months gives about 90 forward observations. Six is what I would want before sizing up. If the forward record disagrees with the backtest, that is the answer and the strategies get retired like the other seven.

Running it

Everything, in order

  1. Start the recorder — LONA-RECORD.bat. Leave the window open. Every day it is not running is order-book data that is gone permanently. Watch that dropped, bad and reconnects all stay at zero.
  2. Build the archive — LONA-PORTFOLIO.bat. Downloads the candles and 1.9M funding records, then prints the study. The funding step loops until complete because the venue rate-limits it.
  3. Check the two rows that matter — the control line must print something absurd like +96,853%, and the t column should read about +1.5 for the breakout. If the control ever looks reasonable, the look-ahead guard has broken and every other row is void.
  4. Run the risk dry run — LONA-RISK.bat. Pushes a sample book through every limit so you can watch each one fire. Nothing is sent.
  5. Verify the tape — Lona.Recorder -- verify on yesterday, every day. Reports the largest gap per stream and checks every counterparty id resolves.
